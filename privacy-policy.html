RainMaker Privacy Policy

Santa Cruz Studios · Effective June 8, 2026

Santa Cruz Studios (“we,” “us”) operates RainMaker Morning. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account: Sign-in identifiers from your auth provider (e.g., Google email/name).
  • Contact preferences: Email and mobile number you enter for alerts (stored in your browser and synced when configured).
  • SMS consent: Timestamp and preference when you check the SMS consent box and save alert settings.
  • Usage: Basic analytics if you opt in to cookies/analytics in Account settings.
  • Trading workflow: Scan imports, trade notes, and journal data you create in the app.

How we use information

  • Provide and improve the Service (scans, charts, research, alerts).
  • Send SMS or email alerts you requested (pre-market briefs, trade alerts, research summaries).
  • Operate broker integrations you connect (e.g., Schwab sync) at your direction.
  • Comply with law and prevent abuse.

SMS program

We send SMS only after express opt-in via the unchecked consent checkbox in Alerts & automations. We do not sell mobile numbers. Reply STOP to opt out; HELP for help. Message frequency about 1–5 texts per trading week. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing

We use service providers to operate the Service (hosting, SMS via Twilio, auth, market data). We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and security

We retain data as needed to provide the Service and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • Update or remove alert preferences in Account settings.
  • Reply STOP to end SMS.
  • Sign out or disconnect integrations (e.g., Schwab) in Account.
  • Contact us to request access or deletion: michaelstewman@gmail.com

Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
